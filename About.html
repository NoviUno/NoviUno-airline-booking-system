Our Story & Purpose

Born from a vision to make inter-island travel effortless, warm, and exceptionally dependable for every Filipino traveler and global explorer.

AIRCRAFT MANIFEST
PR-CB842 "Bayanihan Sky"

First maiden flight dispatched Manila (MNL) to Batanes (BSO), introducing eco-efficient turboprop routes to remote provinces.

Lakbay-Lipad Airlines was founded with a foundational belief: no island in our archipelago should feel out of reach. While standard commercial airlines prioritized high-density trunk routes between major capitals, communities in coastal islands and emerging eco-tourism hubs were left underserved. We pioneered direct, regional connectivity with fuel-efficient fleets optimized for short runways and archipelago climates.

Beyond operational rigor and international safety standards, our soul is rooted in authentic Filipino warmth (Malasakit). From localized in-flight culinary treats celebrating regional flavors to digital-first baggage tracking and community relief flights during monsoons, Lakbay-Lipad exists to lift journeys higher.
